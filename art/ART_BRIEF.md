# First commission · Ironwake

Start with Archer, Cavalry and Horse Archer as one family portrait test. The daughter card should visibly inherit the Archer’s bow and the Cavalry’s teal saddle cloth. The silhouette must read before the player reads the name. Next paint Pikeman and Lancer, then Swordsman and Knight. Deliver the Timber, Armoured and Thorn gates, Palisade and the two battlefields in the same batch.

| Portrait | Visual cue at hand size |
| --- | --- |
| Archer | Bent bow, fletching and a clear crescent of negative space around the bow arm. |
| Cavalry | Unoccluded horse head, sweeping mane and unadorned teal pennant. |
| Horse Archer | Mounted side profile, raised bow and matching saddle cloth. Separate head, bow and reins in silhouette. |
| Pikeman / Lancer | Read the vertical pike first. The Lancer carries the family pennon on horseback. |
| Swordsman / Knight | Bold shield and copper edge. Give the mounted Knight a broader silhouette and heavier armour. |

**World.** Painted frontier citadels, smoky distance, dark iron, warm copper and enamel teal. Give each family its own accent and shape. Keep the picture free of title lettering, supply numerals, UI borders and stats. The game draws those elements.

**Source.** 5:7 portraits at 1080 × 1512 minimum, square fort modules at 1024 × 1024 minimum, battlefields at 2560 × 1440. Keep faces, plumes, hands, bows and hooves inside the central 76% of the canvas. Leave breathing room above the head and a quiet lower fifth for the nameplate. Keep the centre of a battlefield quiet beneath the two lane panels.

**Delivery.** Follow the exact source path in ASSET_MANIFEST.json. Supply the full-resolution source and an approval contact sheet. For this milestone, manually export the approved card window as WebP up to 720 px wide, using the listed path under public/. For backgrounds preserve 16:9, up to 2560 px wide. Record attribution in CREDITS.md, inspect the collector card, hand thumbnail and chip, set the status to approved, then run npm run check:art. Future automated conversion is tracked in the roadmap.

The procedural enamel illustrations are temporary framing proofs. The developer can ship a readable card for any commissioned image that is still in review.

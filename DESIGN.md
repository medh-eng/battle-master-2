# Ironwake duel and forge

Two fronts share one 20 HP citadel. Draw four cards initially and two per later round, without reshuffling. Gain three Supply each round, capped at seven. Players alternate public muster actions until both pass. A player selects excess hand discards above eight. Deploy into lead then rear, or shift an entire formation to the other empty friendly lane once a round. Pay one Supply for that move unless the lead is Mounted.

Lock one order on one lane. Press grants +1 strike or breach. Hold grants 2 Guard on a lead or absorbs two breach on an empty lane. Pulse restores one charge to a depleted lane wall. The rival commits before the human order is submitted.

A contested strike starts at lead attack plus rear Support, printed bonuses and Press, less defender armour. Apply minimum one, then subtract Guard down to zero. An unopposed lead deals two breach plus Press. Resolve empty-lane Hold, the wall, then the central gate against summed breach. Gates counter the largest remaining breach, Road first on ties. Apply all losses simultaneously. After round eight, compare fort HP, then surviving unit HP. Simultaneous destruction and exact ties draw.

A non-final win funds six Marks plus one offered recruit, component or two Marks. At camp, each hybrid consumes two selected basic card instances and three Marks. A gate upgrade consumes the matching component and three Marks. Choose up to two unit fusions and one gate upgrade, obey the eight-unit floor, and optionally buy a recruit or repair. The next clash resets Supply, hands, temporary effects and module charges. Fort HP, army, gate and remaining Marks carry forward.

## First milestone choices

- Seven playable unit definitions, three gate definitions and starting Palisade. Each of the six tactics has its printed rule. The two-stop expedition offers Armour or Spikes according to its seed. Bowcraft and the full rotating reward pool arrive with equipment recipes.
- The Copper Captain fields a legal Horse Archer, Armoured Gate and an eleven-unit army, spending six Marks and an Armour reward. Entry HP matches the player. Standard muster and order selection uses own hand, visible formations and deterministic tie-breaking. Full Veteran search is a later phase.
- The initial save is a compact, versioned localStorage snapshot with a previous-state backup. The release plan includes IndexedDB, migrations and explicit recovery/export. End-of-clash reports are presentation only.
- Original procedural enamel plates stand in for the separate art project. Optimized, approved unit/tactic paintings and battlefield assets replace them by manifest ID. The initial build uses Vite 7, TypeScript 5.9, PixiJS 8 and GSAP 3. Native browser controls supply tap and keyboard input; Pixi renders static scenery on resize and can fall back to Canvas.
